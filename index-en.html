About us

Joldo - a taxi where you name the price

This is an app where passengers and drivers negotiate the price themselves. Taking the best taxi model as a base, we made it uncompromisingly safe and kept the lowest prices on the market.

  • Fair price by agreement
  • No hidden fees
  • No imposed rates
  • Safety first

How it works

  1. Enter where to pick you up and where you are going
  2. Offer how much you are ready to pay for the ride
  3. Drivers respond, and you choose the right one.
  4. Get in the car and arrive at your destination.
